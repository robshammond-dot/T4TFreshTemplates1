The idea: templates power the follow-ups and the key moments in a client's trip. I suggest we start with a small set for the go-live, so the team gets comfortable, then add more in stages. This brings together the CRM Playbook and the Customer Touchpoints map, updated with what we agreed in Cape Town.

1The starter set, for go-live

Seven emails that cover the whole journey, from enquiry to coming home.

1

Thank you, we've got your enquiry

Automatic workflowAs soon as an enquiry comes in

Thanks the client and lets them know their enquiry has arrived, with an honest expectation for when we'll be in touch.

  • Our office hours, stated plainly
  • A real response time, matching what the team can actually do
  • No button needed, it's reassurance

General design. Replaces "Enquiry received" from the CRM Playbook.

2

You're working with [consultant]

Automatic workflowWhen a consultant is assigned to the enquiry

Lets the client know who is looking after their trip. Worded so it works for new and returning clients alike, as many will already know their consultant.

  • Consultant's photo and name
  • What happens next
  • A direct way to contact the consultant

General design. Replaces "Meet your agent" from the CRM Playbook. Assignment stays manual, as the team prefers, so this sends whenever a consultant is set.

3

Quote

Sent by the consultantWhen the quote is ready

Sends the quote in a branded email instead of a plain one. Used by independents and consultants, so it has to be quick: pick the theme that fits the trip, check the details, add the quote, send.

  • Client's name and destination filled in
  • Quote attached or linked
  • A clear "View my quote" button
  • Consultant's name and contact details

Themed. Moved up to go-live.

4

Follow-up

Sales SequenceDay 3 after the quote, if the client hasn't replied

A warm, helpful check-in. It picks up where the conversation left off, without any pressure.

  • Consultant's name attached so it reads as personal
  • On day 5 the consultant gets a reminder to phone the client, so the next step is a conversation, not another email
  • Days include weekends

Themed. Simplified in Cape Town: one email, then a call.

5

Confirmation

Sent by the consultantWhen the booking is confirmed

A branded booking confirmation that independents can send straight from Fresh.

  • Booking reference and travel dates
  • What happens next, and when
  • Consultant's contact details

General design. Moved up to go-live.

6

Bon Voyage

Automatic workflow3 days before departure

The send-off, with a clear checklist so nothing is missed at the last minute.

  • Check in 30 hours before the flight
  • Documents in order
  • Passports and visas
  • Health
  • SARS declaration
  • Travel insurance policy and details
  • Consultant contact details for while they're away

Themed. Checklist agreed in Cape Town.

7

Welcome Home

Automatic workflowA day or two after they return

A genuine, low-pressure check-in, and a short feedback ask.

  • Thank you from the consultant by name
  • A short feedback link
  • A soft "when you're ready" mention of the next trip, no hard sell

Themed. From the CRM Playbook.

Same layout every time

Logo, banner photo, the message, one button, footer. Only the photo and the label change, so the designer builds it once.

Easy to find in Fresh

Each template is named by type then theme, for example "Quote - Beach", so it takes seconds to find.

Personal details

Each email fills in the client's first name, destination, consultant's name and booking reference.

Tone

Warm and helpful, never pushy. No countdowns or "act now" wording.

The sizes and file rules for the designer are in a separate one-page Email Design Brief.
